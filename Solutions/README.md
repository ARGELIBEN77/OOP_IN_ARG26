# Solutions for Self-Checking

Solutions are provided to help you evaluate your reasoning after completing an
exercise or practice assessment.

## Recommended process

1. Complete the work without opening the solution.
2. Test or trace your answer.
3. Compare your reasoning and design decisions with the solution.
4. Record one specific mistake or improvement.
5. Close the solution and correct your answer independently.
