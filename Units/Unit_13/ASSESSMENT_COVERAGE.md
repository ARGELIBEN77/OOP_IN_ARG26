## Interpretation

“Complete” means every stated unit outcome has at least one assessment
opportunity. It does not mean every outcome must appear on one examination.
A formal exam should select a balanced subset while exercises and the review
bank support complete-course preparation.

## Before using a formal examination

Verify its difficulty, point totals, permitted materials, and completion time
against institutional rules. Use a second version with different surface
details so success depends on understanding rather than memorized answers.
